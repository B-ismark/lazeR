## What's new

**Phone**
- **Update from inside the app.** When a newer version is out, **Settings → Updates** has a **Download & install** button. The app downloads the new LazeR.apk, checks it against the release's fingerprint, and hands it to Android to install. Nothing is downloaded until you tap it, and Android still asks you to confirm. This release is the first that can be installed this way. To get it, install 2.3.0 by hand once; later versions can come from inside the app.
- **Tap the speaker icon to mute.** The icon beside Volume is the mute button now. On a Windows laptop running 2.3.0 it turns red with a crossed-out speaker while the laptop is muted, and moving the volume unmutes it, as Windows' own slider does.
- **QR pairing works on more networks.** Campus and office networks that use public or carrier-grade (100.64.x) addresses now pair. Codes that point at no real laptop are still refused.
- **Re-scanning fixes more.** If a laptop was rolled back to an older LazeR, scanning its QR code again is all it takes.
- **Better "can't connect" messages.** If a laptop was re-paired, that message stays up instead of flickering to a generic one. The phone checks its own Wi-Fi before blaming a sleeping laptop, and a VPN or mobile data is no longer mistaken for being on the laptop's network.
- **The laptop list stays current.** Laptops from a network you've left disappear from "Found on your network", including when a VPN is on, and a laptop that was briefly busy is no longer missed.
- **TalkBack** reads each Settings row as one switch with its name.

**Laptop**
- **A safer handshake.** With the 2.3.0 phone, every reply is tied to the phone's own connection attempt, and replayed old messages from someone else on the Wi-Fi can't hijack or block it.
- `LazeR.exe --resume` says whether the running LazeR actually resumed, and exits 0 (resumed), 1 (refused or not running) or 2 (no answer).
- Many smaller reliability and security fixes from a full review of both apps.

Update both halves: `LazeR.exe` on the laptop, `LazeR.apk` on the phone. Either side still works with the other side's older version.
