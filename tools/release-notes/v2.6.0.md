## What's new

**Use your phone as the laptop's microphone** (Windows)
- A new **Mic** switch under the volume slider picks **Laptop** or **Phone**. On Phone, your voice goes to the laptop over the same encrypted link, and Zoom, Teams, Meet or any other app hears it as a normal microphone.
- One-time setup on the laptop: install the free [VB-CABLE](https://vb-audio.com/Cable/) virtual microphone (LazeR → Details → Phone microphone links to it), restart, then pick **CABLE Output** as the microphone in your meeting app. Needs QR pairing.
- Low delay, recovers by itself if the audio device hiccups, and pauses if the phone loses the laptop. A line under the switch tells you what to do in the meeting app.
- Good to know: wear headphones on the laptop (its speakers would echo through the phone). A Bluetooth headset on the phone isn't used; the phone's own mic is.

**Mute the laptop's microphone from the phone**
- A mic button on the volume card mutes and unmutes the laptop's microphone for every app at once, and shows its real state.

Update both `LazeR.exe` on the laptop and the phone app to get these. Either side still works with the other side's older version; the new mic features simply stay hidden.
