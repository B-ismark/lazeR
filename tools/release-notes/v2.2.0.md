## What's new

**Phone**
- **Landscape deck.** Turn the phone sideways for a laptop-style layout: a rail of controls, and Media or Keys docked beside the pad, which is still usable while a panel is open. With a panel open, a compact L / M / R + drag click bar sits under the pad. On narrow windows (split screen, large display size), the panel takes the full width.
- **Typing.** The text field grows as you write and wraps long text. The keyboard's return key types a new line (Shift+Enter), and the Enter button stays one tap away. The on-screen Backspace keeps phone and laptop in step after an emoji, and pasted Windows line breaks no longer submit. Your text survives rotating the phone.
- **Scroll bar on the left** setting.
- **Back gesture.** Strokes started at the pad's edge no longer trigger the phone's Back gesture.
- **Update notices you'll actually see.** A dot on the Settings button when a newer version is out, and **Settings → Updates** now says what the check found (up to date, available, or couldn't check) with a **Check now** button. It also checks when you come back to the app, not only on launch. The Settings sheet scrolls, so nothing at its bottom is cut off sideways.
- Leaving mid-gesture (e.g. rotating while holding drag) releases the mouse button and Alt on the laptop.

**Laptop**
- Quitting always fully exits, and a stuck copy can no longer stop the next launch or leave a dead tray icon behind.
- The single-instance guard ignores callers that hang up instead of briefly giving up its port.

Update both halves: `LazeR.exe` on the laptop, `LazeR.apk` on the phone.

Coming from 2.1.1? That version only shows update notices on the connect screen, which the app skips when it reconnects on launch, so it may never have told you about this release.
